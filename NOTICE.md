## Online services used at runtime

Orbit only links to these services; their content belongs to its respective owners.

- [libretro-thumbnails](https://thumbnails.libretro.com) — box art and screenshots
- [SteamGridDB](https://www.steamgriddb.com) — covers, backgrounds and logos (user's own API key)
- Steam store CDN — artwork for Steam games
- [Homebrew Hub](https://hh.gbdev.io) — free homebrew games (store source)
- [Universal-DB](https://db.universal-team.net) — free 3DS/DS homebrew (store source)
- [Moonlight](https://moonlight-stream.org) — launched for PC streaming
